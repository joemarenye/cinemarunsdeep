Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Cinema Runs Deep (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates cinemarunsdeep.com. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information from visitors. Third-party services we use may automatically collect data including browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and general location data through cookies.

Google AdSense and Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this website. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at google.com/settings/ads.

Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to monitor and analyze website traffic. Google Analytics collects usage data automatically through cookies. For more on how Google uses this data, visit google.com/policies/privacy/partners.

GDPR

If you are a resident of the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. Since we do not directly collect personal data, such requests should be directed to Google through their own privacy controls.

Children's Privacy

Cinema Runs Deep does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
